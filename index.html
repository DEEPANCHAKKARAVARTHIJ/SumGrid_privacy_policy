Privacy Policy for Sumloria: Puzzle Grid

Last Updated: November 8, 2026

Brain Burst Studio develops and publishes Sumloria: Puzzle Grid. This Privacy Policy informs players and guardians about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our Game.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Sumloria: Puzzle Grid is designed to respect your privacy:

  • No Account Required: No login, registration, or real name required.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: No camera, microphone, contacts, storage files, or GPS location access.
  • No Financial Information: We do not collect credit card or banking information.

2. Local Storage

All progress, completion stats, daily streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device in an encrypted offline Room database. This data never leaves your device.

3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

The game displays non-intrusive banner and optional rewarded ads powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may process non-personal diagnostic data and advertising identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Sumloria is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

For questions or support, contact Brain Burst Studio at support@brainburst.studio.
