# Privacy Policy for Sumloria: Puzzle Grid

**Last Updated:** November 8, 2026

**Brain Burst Studio** ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes **Sumloria: Puzzle Grid** ("the Game", "the App", package: `com.sumloria.numberpuzzle`). This Privacy Policy informs players and guardians about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our Game.

By downloading, installing, or playing Sumloria: Puzzle Grid, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Sumloria: Puzzle Grid is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy:
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide your real name, email, or phone number to play.
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not request or access your camera, microphone, contacts, files, or precise GPS location.
- **No Financial Information:** We do not collect credit card or banking information.

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless offline gameplay experience, the App stores game state data locally on your device using an offline Room SQLite database:
- Current level progress, solved puzzles, and completion stars
- Daily Challenge completion status and streak counter
- User preferences (Dark Mode, High Contrast, Mistake Checking, Sound Effects, Vibration)

This information stays strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The App uses Google AdMob to display non-intrusive banner and optional rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may process non-personal diagnostic data and advertising identifiers (AAID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

- **Google Privacy Policy:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob Information:** [https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

Players in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, allowing complete control over personalized vs. non-personalized advertising.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Sumloria: Puzzle Grid is a family-friendly number logic puzzle designed for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
